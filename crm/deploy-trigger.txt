PEPTIVANTA CRM production redeploy trigger
2026-08-26T08:02:00+08:00
Added a formal 客户删除 permission. Team leaders receive it by default through a one-time permission migration and may delete only customers inside their team scope; superadmins retain full permission and other roles can be configured through the permission matrix. Customer deletion is blocked when linked orders exist, every successful deletion is audited, and delete controls are shown only to authorized users in the workbench/customer detail UI.
